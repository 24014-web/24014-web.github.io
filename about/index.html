About AI Athena

New Zealand made. Built for thoughtful questions.

AI Athena was made by Zander Glinkowski, proudly New Zealand made and owned. Built by a student at Rosmini College for students and everyone else, Athena is a straightforward place to ask questions, explore ideas, and learn with care.

It is designed to be useful without pretending to be perfect: important answers should always be checked against reliable sources.
