Privacy policy · last updated 24 August 2026

Your chats are yours.

What we collect

We collect your email address, securely hashed password, account identifiers, saved conversations, messages, and basic technical information needed to operate and protect the site, including session and security data.

How we use and share it

We use this information to provide the account and chat service, keep it secure, and respond to support requests. Account and chat records are stored through Appwrite Cloud in the Sydney region. Prompts are sent to the Azure AI service configured for Athena to generate a response. These providers may process data outside New Zealand under their applicable terms and safeguards. Do not include sensitive personal, health, financial, or confidential information in prompts.

Retention and deletion

Chats remain in your account until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting your account deletes the account and chat records held by AI Athena. Provider systems may retain data according to their own policies.

Your rights

You can request access to or correction of your personal information, or ask privacy questions, at zndr365@gmail.com. This policy is intended to support the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020; it is not legal advice.

Cookies and advertising

We use essential session and security cookies. If Google advertising is enabled, Google and other third parties may use cookies, IP addresses, or similar identifiers for ad delivery. We will keep this policy updated with any analytics or advertising changes.
